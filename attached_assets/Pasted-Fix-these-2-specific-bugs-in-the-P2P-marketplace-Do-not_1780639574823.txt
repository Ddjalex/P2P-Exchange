BUG 1 — Buy/Sell ads showing on wrong tab
The problem:
When a user posts a Buy ad it shows on the Buy tab — this is WRONG.
When a user posts a Sell ad it shows on the Sell tab — this is WRONG.
Why it's wrong (MEXC logic):
The Buy tab shows ads from people who want to SELL USDT to you.
The Sell tab shows ads from people who want to BUY USDT from you.
The fix:
BUY tab in marketplace → fetch ads where type = 'sell'
SELL tab in marketplace → fetch ads where type = 'buy'
In src/pages/p2p.tsx find where ads are fetched and fix the query:
typescript// When user is on BUY tab:
fetch('/api/ads?type=sell&status=online')

// When user is on SELL tab:
fetch('/api/ads?type=buy&status=online')
Logic explanation:

I posted a SELL ad = I am selling USDT = other users see it on their BUY tab
I posted a BUY ad = I am buying USDT = other users see it on their SELL tab
