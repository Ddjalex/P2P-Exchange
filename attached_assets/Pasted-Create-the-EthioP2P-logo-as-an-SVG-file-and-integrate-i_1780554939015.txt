Step 4 — Use logo across the app
In index.html:
html<link rel="icon" type="image/svg+xml" href="/src/assets/favicon.svg">
<title>EthioP2P — Ethiopia's P2P Crypto Exchange</title>
In every page header/navbar — replace any text logo with:
tsximport LogoBanner from "@/assets/logo-banner.svg";

<img src={LogoBanner} alt="EthioP2P" height={44} />
In the Auth page (auth_v2.html) — replace the text logo div:
html<!-- Remove this: -->
<div class="logo">Ethio<span>P2P</span></div>

<!-- Replace with: -->
<img src="/src/assets/logo-banner.svg" alt="EthioP2P" height="52" style="margin-bottom:24px;">
In the Admin panel sidebar — use icon only:
tsximport LogoIcon from "@/assets/logo-icon.svg";

<img src={LogoIcon} alt="EthioP2P" width={40} height={40} />
In the browser tab:
html<link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/src/assets/logo-icon.svg">

Logo Usage Rules (add as comments in the SVG files)
- Minimum size: banner 160px wide, icon 32px
- Always on dark background (#1a1a2e or #0f1929)
- Never change colors — always #00d4ff cyan + white
- Never stretch or distort
- Clear space: minimum 8px around all sides
