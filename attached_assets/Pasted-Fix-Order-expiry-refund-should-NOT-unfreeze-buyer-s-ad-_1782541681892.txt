Fix: Order expiry refund should NOT unfreeze buyer's ad balance
Current broken behavior:

Buyer creates buy ad with 500 USDT frozen
Seller accepts order but doesn't pay within 15 minutes
System refunds USDT after 15 min ✅
BUT system also unfreezes the balance and re-activates the ad ❌
Ad becomes available again automatically — risky!

Correct behavior wanted:

Buyer creates buy ad with 500 USDT frozen
Seller accepts order but doesn't pay within 15 minutes
System cancels the order ✅
USDT is refunded back to buyer's available balance ✅
BUT the buy ad should be set to PAUSED/INACTIVE status ❌→✅
Buyer must manually re-activate their ad when ready
Frozen balance is released back to available balance ✅

typescript// When order expires (seller didn't pay):

// ❌ This releases frozen balance and ad stays active

// Fix — also pause the ad:
await db.update(orders).set({ status: 'cancelled', cancelReason: 'Payment timeout' });
