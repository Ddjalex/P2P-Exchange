Critical Fix: Seller's USDT balance not frozen when creating sell ad
When a seller creates a sell ad, their USDT must be frozen immediately to prevent them from spending the same USDT elsewhere.
Current broken behavior:

Seller has $500 USDT available balance
Seller creates sell ad for $500 USDT
Balance stays at $500 available — NOT frozen ❌
Seller could create multiple ads or withdraw the same USDT ❌

Correct behavior:

Seller has $500 USDT available balance
Seller creates sell ad for $500 USDT
$500 moves from available → frozen immediately ✅
Available balance shows $0 ✅
Frozen balance shows $500 ✅


Fix in POST /api/ads/create (or wherever ads are created):
